NOCTURNIS: HARVEST OF FALLEN STARS — 0.8.4

ACT I ASSET LIBRARY
62 models prepared for Nocturnis: 32 reference-based 3D reconstructions using imagegen and TRELLIS.2, plus 30 models created in Blender. Materials, rigs, weights, weapon grips and procedural animation work: the Nocturnis project.
TRELLIS.2 — Microsoft, https://github.com/microsoft/TRELLIS.2
Blender — Blender Foundation and contributors, https://www.blender.org/
